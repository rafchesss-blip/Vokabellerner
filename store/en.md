# Beschreibung (max. 4000 Zeichen)

Learn vocabulary in any language – with flashcards, tests and smart review. Free, no ads, no in-app purchases and no account.

📷 SCAN YOUR VOCAB INSTEAD OF TYPING IT
Take a photo of the word list in your textbook. An AI app of your choice (e.g. ChatGPT or Gemini, the free version works too) reads it for you. Copy the answer, come back – done. Of course, you can also type in the words yourself.

📚 YOUR OWN LESSONS IN ANY LANGUAGE
English, French, Latin, Spanish or whatever you're learning right now: you decide what belongs to a word – the word and its translation, plus forms if you like (principal parts, plural, gender, comparison and more) and up to three custom fields such as an example sentence or a memory aid.

🧠 SMART REVIEW
Every word comes back right before you would forget it (spaced repetition with the FSRS algorithm). Words you know well come up less often, tricky ones more often.

🎯 READY FOR YOUR VOCAB TEST
Enter the date of your test. The app spreads the new words over the days before it and reviews everything once more on the last day.

✍️ FLASHCARDS AND TEST MODE
Flip flashcards and rate yourself – or type in the answers just like in a real test.

🔔 REMINDERS
Up to three times a day, with your current numbers – never after 9 pm. Once you've learned everything, it stays quiet.

🔒 EVERYTHING STAYS ON YOUR PHONE
No account, no cloud, no tracking. Your lessons and your progress aren't stored anywhere on the internet. Only when you scan does the photo go to the AI app you choose yourself.

🌍 IN 10 LANGUAGES
German, English, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese, Polish, Turkish, Russian and Chinese.

Vocab Trainer is not affiliated with OpenAI, Google or any other provider of AI apps.
