# Privacy Policy – Vokabeltrainer

Last updated: 11/11/2026

Vokabeltrainer is free. There are no ads, no in-app purchases,
no subscription and no account. No money is made with the app.

## What data the app stores

Your lessons, words, practice lists, your learning progress and – if you
enter it – your name are stored **only on your device**. They are not sent
to us or to anyone else. There is no server, no analytics tools and no
tracking.

If you delete the app, the data is gone too. Under “Settings → Data &
backup” you can create a backup yourself.

## Scanning with AI

When you use “Scan”, you take a photo of your vocabulary list. The app
then opens the AI app you choose yourself (e.g. ChatGPT or Gemini) with
this photo and instructions. This only happens when you trigger it
yourself. The processing in that app is governed by its own privacy
policy. Vokabeltrainer is not part of these apps and is not affiliated
with their providers.

So that the AI's answer can come back, Vokabeltrainer reads the
clipboard. This only happens while you are waiting for an answer or
when you tap “Paste answer”.

## Permissions

- **Notifications:** for study reminders. They are scheduled on the
  device, without internet.
- **Run at startup:** so that scheduled reminders are kept after the
  phone restarts.
- **Camera / photos:** only when scanning. The selection is handled by the
  system; the app only receives that one photo.
- **See installed AI apps:** The app only checks which of the known
  AI apps are installed so they can be offered when scanning.
